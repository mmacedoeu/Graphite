# wireframe-progressive-build — preset card

AnimationTime progresses a 4-second build: line strokes paint from the
bottom-left corner; rectangular solids fill as the lines complete; the
final frame holds for 0.5s before looping.

Aliases: blueprint-build.

Required: AnimationTimeNode, RectangleNode, LineNode, StrokeNode, FillNode.

Best for: technical explainers, real-estate renders, before/after reveals.
