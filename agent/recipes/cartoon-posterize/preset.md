# cartoon-posterize — preset card

PosterizeReduce the source to 4-5 color bands, then smooth with a 3-pixel
median to keep edges clean.

Aliases: toon-shader, comic-flatten.

Required: PosterizeShaderNodeNode, MedianFilterNode, ThresholdNode.

Best for: stylised photo illustrations, marketing cards, motion-graphic key
frames that need to read on a small thumbnail.
