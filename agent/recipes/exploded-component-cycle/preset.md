# exploded-component-cycle — preset card

Four components explode from the centre at t=0, hold their offsets, then
return to the centre by t=6. 30 fps, seamless loop, identical positioning
on every loop.

Aliases: component-cycle.

Required: AnimationTimeNode, StrokeNode, OpacityNode, RectangleNode, LineNode.

Best for: hardware explainers, product anatomy clips, marketing motion cards.
