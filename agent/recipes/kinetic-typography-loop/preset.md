# kinetic-typography-loop — preset card

Headline builds word-by-word from frame 0 to frame 30, holds at the
golden-ratio break, kern-snaps the centre pair at frame 90, and resets over
the last 30 frames. 4 seconds, 30 fps, seamless loop.

Aliases: animated-type, loop-type.

Required: AnimationTimeNode, TextToVectorNode, RectangleNode, OpacityNode.

Best for: launch teasers, marketing-card animation, social-loop hero clips.
