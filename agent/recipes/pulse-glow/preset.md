# pulse-glow — preset card

A real-time clock feeds a sin-wave; BrightnessContrast modulates the subject
on every frame, producing a 2-second breathing pulse.

Aliases: breathe, sin-pulse.

Required: RealTimeNode, BrightnessContrastShaderNodeNode, MathNode, SinNode.

Best for: live indicator badges, hero CTAs, hover-state animations.
